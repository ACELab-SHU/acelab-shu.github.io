Personal Profile

Biography

Zeyu Hu received his Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2023. From 2022 to 2023, he was a visiting doctoral student at The University of Electro-Communications, Japan. He is currently a faculty member at the School of Communication and Information Engineering, Shanghai University.

His research interests include space-air-ground integrated networks, wireless communications, multi-agent collaboration, and integrated sensing and communications. He serves as a reviewer for leading journals including IEEE Transactions on Communications, China Communications, and the Journal of Communications and Networks. He has led or participated in several national and provincial research projects and published more than ten papers in peer-reviewed journals and international conferences.
